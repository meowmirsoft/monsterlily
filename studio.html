Kasha Club

Kasha Club is a small studio, really small. If you count cats, there are currently three mostly full-time staff. Maybe if Monster Lily is successful there will be more, but in the meantime we are focused entirely on Monster Lily and that will be the case for the foreseeable future.

For questions, media, inquiries, or even to report a problem with Monster Lily please send an email to Kasha Club or reach out on discord.

Collaborators

RyanAvx - Guest Composer Highlight
This spotlight is on RyanAvx, composer for much of the music in Monster Lily. His tracks really set a special "feel" in Monster Lily. I'm not sure, is this the first ascii game with a full soundtrack? His sound and his excellent catalog were a big inspiration for the game’s theme. On top of that, Monster Lily includes several custom tracks from this extremely versatile and talented composer. We're really fortunate to get to collaborate with him. Please check him out!


Keegan Hoffer - Guest Artist Highlight
Geochemical creative and scientist. On somewhat of a chance encounter we connected on the idea of symbolic graphics, something once thought not possible due to the game's limited tile resolution. Keegan's novel ideation and contributions to Monster Lily allows the game to be enjoyed with more emblematic glyphs than the original ASCII. The new "icon graphics" will appear in Monster Lily's 1.1 version!
